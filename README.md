# SB 79 Transit-Oriented Development Analysis (Los Angeles)

Geospatial eligibility analysis of the City of Los Angeles under California's SB 79, with equity and risk overlays, delivered as a StoryMap and a policy brief.

## Problem

SB 79 allows denser housing near qualifying transit stops. This project asks which zoning areas in the City of Los Angeles are eligible, and how those areas line up with opportunity, displacement risk, environmental burden, and fire hazard. Built as a two-person group research project for Introduction to Urban Data Analytics (City and Regional Planning) at UC Berkeley, submitted July 2, 2026.

## Approach

- Built a GIS eligibility engine in ArcGIS Online that flags zoning features near qualifying transit stops.
- Overlaid four equity and risk layers on the eligible set: TCAC/HCD opportunity areas, displacement-risk tracts, CalEnviroScreen 4.0, and CAL FIRE fire hazard zones.
- Wrote up the analysis as a research paper, a StoryMap, and a policy brief.
- My part: Methods, Results, Discussion (limitations), Abstract, and Appendices sections of the paper.

## Result

The final eligible layer contains **6,413 zoning features** near qualifying transit stops, each characterized against the four equity and risk layers. Findings and limitations are written up in the policy brief.
